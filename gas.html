About these prices

  • Source. AAA Indiana state page is the upstream. The TCG home server scrapes 15 metros every 30 minutes and stores 14 days of history.
  • Seymour. Not its own AAA metro. Columbus, IN (16.8 miles) is the closest reading. Bloomington (37.0 mi) and Indianapolis (57.7 mi) round out the 60-mile circle.
  • Predictions. Best-effort heuristic from oil futures and seasonality. We don’t guarantee anything — gas markets can move on a single OPEC headline.
  • Refresh. This page polls live every time you load it and again whenever you tab back. The "Last updated" chip flips to red if data is more than 6 hours stale.
