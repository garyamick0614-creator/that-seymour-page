Walmart Supercenter (Seymour)

"The single most-trafficked building in Jackson County." — not officially measured, but functionally true

Category: Retail / Supercenter · 1600 E Tipton St, Seymour, IN 47274

Quick take. The Tipton Street Walmart Supercenter is Seymour's everyday everything store: groceries, pharmacy, auto care, optical, banking partner, and the de facto town square at 9 PM on a Tuesday. Loved for prices and convenience, persistently complained about for understaffed checkouts, theft-prevention friction, and parking-lot incidents. Same Walmart story as everywhere; the local store does it slightly better than national average on Google reviews.

Who they are

The Seymour Walmart Supercenter is a standard-format Walmart store at 1600 E Tipton Street, opened on the Tipton commercial corridor where most of Seymour's chain retail clusters. Format includes full grocery (produce, meat, dairy, bakery, frozen, packaged), full general merchandise, pharmacy, eye care, auto care center, McDonald's in-store partner, and Walmart Pickup. Open 6 AM-11 PM most days (hours subject to change — check the store's Google listing).

Owned and operated by Walmart Inc. (NYSE: WMT), headquartered in Bentonville, Arkansas. Local store leadership reports up through the regional and divisional structure. The Seymour store is supplied substantially out of the local Walmart Distribution Center 6088 on the west side of town — same corporate parent, separate operations.

What they do well

Pricing and availability. This is the Walmart value proposition and Seymour's store delivers it. Grocery prices undercut Kroger and IGA on most staples; general merchandise undercuts most non-Walmart options on most categories. For a household stretching a paycheck (which is the median Seymour household), the Tipton Walmart is the single highest-leverage retail destination in town.

Pickup and delivery. Walmart's grocery pickup service works well at this store, and delivery is available within the local radius. For working parents, this is a real time-saver and reviewers consistently call it out as a positive.

Pharmacy. Walmart Pharmacy at this location is well-reviewed compared to other big-box pharmacies. Generic-drug pricing through the Walmart $4 generic list is real and accepted. The pharmacy team is generally well-rated for service in public reviews.

Auto care. Tire installation, oil changes, basic maintenance. Pricing is competitive and generally faster than the dealerships if you're not under warranty.

What people complain about

Self-checkout dominance and understaffed registers. Like Walmart stores nationally, the Seymour store has shifted heavily toward self-checkout and the staffed-register count at peak times can be very low. Reviewers frequently complain about long waits with full carts forced into self-checkout. This is a corporate strategy, not a local-store decision, but customers experience it locally.

Theft-prevention friction. Receipt checking at exits, locked merchandise cases (electronics, certain personal care items, baby formula), and security challenges have all increased over the past several years. Reviewers describe these as either reasonable response to retail theft or as offensive treatment of paying customers, depending on the reviewer.

Cleanliness inconsistency. Reviews split — the store gets praised for being well-stocked and clean by some, dinged for messy aisles, broken displays, and bathroom issues by others. Standard pattern for a high-volume Supercenter where cleaning crews are running against constant traffic.

Parking lot incidents. Police reports periodically reference Seymour Walmart for shoplifting, vehicle break-ins, and occasional more serious incidents. This is true of essentially every Walmart Supercenter in the country and reflects volume, not a Seymour-specific problem.

How they handle it

Walmart's customer service line and the store's customer service desk handle returns and complaints. The return policy is industry-leading for major categories — if you have the receipt and the item is within window, you'll get the refund. Without a receipt the experience is more variable. Pharmacy issues escalate through Walmart's pharmacist-on-duty and corporate pharmacy chain. The store has visible loss-prevention staff and works with Seymour PD on parking-lot incidents.

Local impact

The Seymour Supercenter employs roughly 250-350 associates between full-time, part-time, and the in-store partners. It pays the largest single retail-property tax bill in Seymour. It draws shopping from a wide rural radius — people from Brownstown, Crothersville, Vallonia, and surrounding Jackson and Lawrence County drive to this Walmart. That's economic capture for Seymour but also displacement: smaller local retailers in surrounding towns lose business to it. That tradeoff is the Walmart effect documented across rural America.

Bottom line

7 / 10

Walmart is Walmart. The Seymour Supercenter is a competently-run example of the format — better than the worst Walmarts you've been to, not as polished as the newest. For the household budget it's the right answer for a lot of shopping; for a complete-meals grocery trip with quality produce or a dignified shopping experience without locked cases and self-checkout lines, you have other options in Seymour. Most people use both.
